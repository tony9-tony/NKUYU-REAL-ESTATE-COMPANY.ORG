## The company
- MKUYU Africa is a Tanzanian real estate company. Tagline: "Quality homes. Real ownership."
- Vision: one million modern, affordable homes across Tanzania.
- CEO: Juneid Othman.
- Office: Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.
- No public phone number or e-mail is published yet. Visitors reach MKUYU through the Contact page (contact.html); the team replies.

## What MKUYU offers
- Property development: homes, apartments and mixed developments.
- Property sales: verified titles, clear pricing, written terms agreed before any money changes hands.
- Property rental: rental homes with proper agreements for owner and tenant.
- Sell with MKUYU: owners can offer their property; the sales team reviews it.
- Flexible payment plans: instalment arrangements on applicable terms, agreed in writing in the contract.
- Title and due diligence: ownership documents, contract terms, location and payment terms explained before you commit.
- Miliki Ardhi Diaspora: helps Tanzanians living abroad own property in Tanzania while still overseas.

## How it works for a visitor (no account is needed for any of these)
- To rent: open the Rent page (rent.html), choose a home, press Request and leave name, phone, e-mail and budget. The MKUYU team contacts you, arranges a viewing, and the rent and terms are written in a rental agreement.
- To buy: open the Buy page (buy.html), choose a home, press Request and leave your details. The team contacts you, arranges a viewing, and the price and the payment plan (full payment or instalments) are written in a sale contract that both sides sign.
- To sell your property: fill in the Sell form (sell.html) with the property type, location, asking price and your contact details. The sales team reviews it, Customer Service calls you about the next steps, and if MKUYU accepts the property the terms are agreed in a contract.
- Projects (projects.html) are developments with homes for sale.
- General questions: the Contact page (contact.html).
- Payments: how and where to pay is given to the customer by MKUYU together with the contract. A receipt is issued for every payment. The assistant never gives account numbers.
- Questions about your own contract, payments or receipts are answered by the MKUYU team directly, through the Contact page.
